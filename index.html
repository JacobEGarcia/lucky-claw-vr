LUCKY CLAW

a hand-inked claw machine, life size · colorfigures · quest 3 · webxr

Quest 3: open this page in the headset browser and tap ENTER VR. Either thumbstick steers the claw, trigger drops it. Point at the paper buttons and pull the trigger for INSERT COIN and START OVER.

On a screen: click PLAY, then steer with the arrow keys (or WASD), Space to drop. Every play costs a token; the claw slips sometimes, like a real one. Third try never misses.
